The Fusion Forge is V Rising's endgame min-max station — and it's also the easiest place in the game to accidentally delete a weapon you spent hours farming. If you're pushing into the Oakveil Woodlands endgame and wondering whether the grind is worth it, here's the honest breakdown from 1,200+ hours in Vardoran.

The one rule nobody explains: the right slot is destroyed

The Fusion Forge has two input slots, and they are not equal:

  • Left slot — the item that survives.
  • Right slot — the item that's destroyed.

Put your best Ancestral weapon on the right and the forge happily feeds it to a worse one on the left. There's no undo once you confirm. The forge does show you the merged attribute list before anything locks in, so always double-check your slots before hitting confirm.

It doesn't actually craft weapons

This is where new players get lost. The Fusion Forge does not craft weapons, and it does not repair broken ones.

If you're holding a shattered Legendary weapon shard and hoping to turn it into something usable, you're at the wrong station. That's the Ancestral Forge's job — it restores shattered shards for 3 Onyx Tears, and it's unlocked by a different boss (Raziel the Shepherd, Lv 57).

The Fusion Forge only works on already-restored, usable Ancestral weapons and jewels. Restore first, fuse second.

What fusing actually does

Fusion merges two Ancestral weapons (or two jewels for the same spell) into one attribute list, then you pick what survives:

  1. Both attribute lists are merged.
  2. You choose which attributes the final item keeps.
  3. The result keeps the same number of attributes as the left-slot item — you're swapping better attributes in, not stacking more.

On shared attributes, only the higher-level version is offered, so you can't double up by fusing two items with the same affix.

The real cost: Ember Glass

Everyone plans around the unlock. Nobody budgets the fuel. Each fusion costs 12 Ember Glass (9 with Forge Flooring), on top of the 12 you spend building the thing. Ember Glass is 8 Venom Sap + 20 Emery at the Advanced Furnace.

Two farming notes worth knowing:

  • Emery nodes in the northwest Oakveil Woodlands explode if you mine them too fast — slow down or you lose the node.
  • Venom Sap drops from Corrupted Treants and the Carvers Logging Outpost.

How to unlock it

The gate is a single fight: Dantos the Forgebinder, a Lv 82 V Blood in the Venom Blades Bastion in the Oakveil Woodlands. Killing him unlocks both the forge and the Ember Glass recipe.

The full build cost is 1 Primal Blood Essence + 12 Ember Glass + 16 Dark Silver Ingot. The Primal Blood Essence is the sneaky ingredient — it's refined at the Blood Press, not dropped.

The bottom line

Respect two rules and the Fusion Forge is pure upside: keeper on the left, donor on the right, and Ember Glass is the real grind, not the unlock.
