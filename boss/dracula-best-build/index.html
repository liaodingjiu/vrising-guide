V Rising Dracula Build — Best Setup for the Final Boss

Dracula is the final boss of V Rising — GS 91, 3 phases, the hardest fight in the game. The exact build, strategy, and phase-by-phase tactics to beat him.

Table of Contents

  • Pre-Fight Requirements
  • Greatsword Brute Build (Melee)
  • Longbow Scholar Build (Range)
  • Phase-by-Phase Strategy
  • Common Mistakes & Recovery

Pre-Fight Requirements

Dracula has a Gear Score requirement of 91 — the highest in the game. You cannot enter his throne room below this threshold. Being under-geared even by a single point locks you out entirely, so make sure every slot is at endgame tier before attempting the fight.

Gear Checklist

  • Sanguine Armor Set (full set required) — This is the endgame armor tier, upgraded from the Dracula-line progression path. No substitutes work; the GS math demands the full Sanguine set across all four armor slots.
  • Legendary Weapon — Your weapon must be Legendary-tier to hit GS 91. A Sanguine-crafted weapon or an upgraded ancestral Legendary both work. Greatsword and Longbow are the two best picks for this fight (detailed below).
  • Accessories — Sanguine-tier accessories across all three slots. Prioritize physical crit chance (melee) or spell crit chance (ranged), depending on your chosen build.

Blood Type

  • 100% Brute Blood — Recommended for the melee build. Provides 25% increased attack speed, significant bonus health, and health leech on melee hits. The attack speed bonus is what makes greatsword feel responsive despite its normally slow swing speed.
  • 100% Scholar Blood — Recommended for the ranged build. Reduces spell cooldowns on spell hit, adds spell leech, and grants bonus spell power. The cooldown reset mechanic lets you chain Chaos Volleys for sustained ranged pressure.

Potions & Consumables

  • Fire Resistance Potion — Mandatory. Many of Dracula's Phase 2 and Phase 3 attacks deal fire damage. The fire resistance potion cuts that damage by roughly 30%. Do not skip this.
  • Physical Power Potion — Boosts overall damage output by 8% for melee builds. Stack with Brute blood's attack speed for maximum burst.
  • Spell Power Potion — The ranged-build equivalent. Boosts spell damage by 8% for Scholar builds.
  • Max Health Potion — Gives you the margin to survive one extra hit, which is often the difference between a wipe and a kill.
  • Blood Rose Brew Potions (10-15) — Your primary healing consumable. Bring at least 10, preferably 15. You will burn through multiple pots per phase, especially while learning the fight's timing.

Arena Preparation

Before pulling Dracula, clear all surrounding mobs in the throne room approach corridor. Dracula's arena is a closed instance, but stray patrols can aggro through walls if you kite too close to the entrance. Set your spawn point at the nearest Vampire Waygate (Throne Room approach) so a death doesn't mean a five-minute run-back. Consider placing a spare set of potions in a nearby castle chest as a backup restock point — running out of Blood Rose Brew mid-fight with Dracula at 5% is a uniquely painful experience.

Pro Tip: Death runs are the #1 time sink on Dracula. Bind your Waygate before the fight and confirm it works. A 30-second return beats a 5-minute corpse run every time.

Greatsword Brute Build (Melee)

The Greatsword Brute build is the highest burst-damage option against Dracula. It relies on landing massive charged heavy attacks during Dracula's recovery windows and using Veil of Blood to sustain through his pressure phases. This build rewards aggressive, disciplined play — you need to know exactly when to commit and when to disengage. If you mistime a single charged heavy, Dracula punishes with a 3-hit combo that can one-shot you through Sanguine armor.

Slot Choice Reason
Weapon Legendary Greatsword Maximum single-hit burst damage. The charged heavy attack can hit for 600+ on a crit, letting you exploit Dracula's short recovery windows efficiently. The Q-spin (Whirlwind) doubles as AoE clear for Phase 2 wolf packs.
Blood 100% Brute 25% attack speed amplifies greatsword DPS dramatically — it turns the normally sluggish greatsword into a responsive weapon. Bonus health and health leech provide the sustain needed to stay in melee range without constantly backing off to heal.
Armor Sanguine Set Required for GS 91. The set bonus enhances physical damage, which directly benefits greatsword charged heavies and Q-spin ticks.
Travel Spell Veil of Blood Dash that heals you on passing through enemies. Use it aggressively — Veil through Dracula to reposition behind him while healing simultaneously. This is your primary sustain tool and should be used on cooldown during Phase 1 and Phase 2.
Spell 1 Sanguine Coil Ranged sustain tool. Throw it during disengage phases to keep your health topped off. Targets both Dracula and any adds for healing return. Lands reliably at mid-range, making it your go-to when forced out of melee.
Spell 2 Chaos Volley Ranged poke for when Dracula forces you out of melee range. Use during his AoE phases or when you're forced to disengage and wait out an attack pattern. Two projectiles per cast, both benefit from Brute blood's leech.
Ultimate Heart Strike Massive single-target burst plus a substantial heal. Save this for Phase 3 when Dracula's blood shield is up — the burst helps break the shield, and the heal buys you crucial extra survival time during his most dangerous phase.

Combo Rotation

The core melee loop: Veil through Dracula → Q-spin (Whirlwind) → dodge backward → charged heavy attack during his recovery frames. Here's the detailed breakdown: Dracula telegraphs most sword combos with a brief pause and a slight crouch. Veil through him as he swings — you get i-frames during the dash plus a heal on contact. Immediately Q-spin for 3-4 ticks of damage while positioned behind him. Then dodge backward once to create space. As he recovers from his swing animation (roughly 1-2 seconds), begin charging your heavy attack. Release the charged heavy as his recovery ends. Do not auto-attack spam between rotations; the greatsword's value is in charged heavies, not light attack DPS. If Dracula begins a new attack chain before your heavy finishes charging, abort with a dodge — a partial charge is not worth taking a full sword combo.

Key Rule for Melee: Two hits maximum per punish window, then dodge out. Dracula's follow-up sword swipe comes out faster than you think. Greed = death. This is not hyperbole — his 3-hit combo deals roughly 85% of your HP through Sanguine armor with full potion buffs active.

Longbow Scholar Build (Range)

The Longbow Scholar build is the safer, more consistent option — especially if you struggle with Dracula's melee pressure. You stay at maximum range, punish with charged bow shots and Chaos Volley, and use Frost Barrier to negate his gap-closing charge attacks. Lower theoretical DPS than the greatsword build, but significantly more forgiving of positioning mistakes. If you're dying to sword combos in Phase 1, switch to this build.

Slot Choice Reason
Weapon Legendary Longbow Safest range option by a wide margin. Charged shots deal high single-target damage from outside Dracula's entire sword range. The AoE rain-of-arrows ability clears Phase 2 wolf adds efficiently without forcing you into melee.
Blood 100% Scholar Cooldown resets on spell hits mean more frequent Chaos Volley casts — often two volleys back-to-back if both projectiles connect. Spell leech provides sustain from your constant spell damage output, reducing reliance on Blood Rose Brew.
Armor Sanguine Set Required for GS 91. Scholar blood benefits from the set's spell power bonus, which amplifies Chaos Volley, Frost Barrier's retaliatory damage, and Raging Tempest.
Travel Spell Veil of Blood Emergency escape tool. Unlike the melee build, you use this defensively — Veil away from Dracula when he closes the gap, not through him. The heal on contact gives you a small top-up as you retreat to max range.
Spell 1 Chaos Volley Primary damage spell. Cast on cooldown. Scholar blood's cooldown reset mechanic means you can often fire two volleys back-to-back if both projectiles connect, effectively doubling your spell damage output during punish windows.
Spell 2 Frost Barrier Blocks Dracula's charge attacks and teleport strikes entirely. Pop Barrier the moment you see him crouch or blur — it absorbs the hit and freezes him for a 2-second punish window. This single spell neutralizes his most dangerous gap-closer.
Ultimate Raging Tempest AoE storm that clears wolf adds in Phase 2 and deals sustained damage to Dracula while you reposition. Pairs excellently with the longbow's rain-of-arrows for add phases. Cast centered on yourself to create a safe zone wolves cannot cross.

Playstyle & Positioning

Stay at maximum bow range at all times — roughly the radius of Dracula's throne dais. This distance is far enough that his sword swings whiff entirely but close enough that your charged shots don't suffer damage falloff. The combat loop is straightforward: charged bow shot → Chaos Volley → reposition → repeat. When Dracula dashes toward you (telegraphed by a crouch animation), pop Frost Barrier immediately — it completely negates the charge and roots him in place. Use the 2-second freeze window to land a fully-charged headshot for bonus damage. During Phase 2, save Raging Tempest for the wolf packs; cast it centered on yourself, and the storm will wipe all wolves within its radius while you continue damaging Dracula with charged shots from within the storm's safety zone.

Key Rule for Range: If you can see Dracula's eye glow, you're too close. The longbow out-ranges every one of his attacks except the teleport strike — and Frost Barrier handles that one. Maintain distance religiously, and the fight becomes a methodical DPS check rather than a reaction-speed test.

Phase-by-Phase Strategy

Dracula has three distinct phases, triggered by his health thresholds (70% and 30%). Each phase escalates in aggression, introduces new mechanics, and shortens your safe punish windows. The table below breaks down exactly what to expect and how to respond in each phase.

Phase Dracula HP Mechanics Strategy
Phase 1 100-70% Sword combos (3-hit slash chain), teleport strike (reappears behind you after vanishing), blood orbs (slow-moving projectiles that heal Dracula for ~5% HP each if they reach him). This is the learning phase — Dracula's attack patterns are at their most predictable here. Dodge sword swings by side-stepping (not back-dodging — he lunges forward and back-dodging keeps you in range). After his teleport strike, he has a roughly 2-second recovery window where he stands still — this is your primary DPS window. Destroy blood orbs immediately. Each orb heals Dracula for approximately 5% of his max HP if it connects. Letting even 3 orbs through effectively resets a full minute of damage. Use ranged attacks or AoE to clear orbs while maintaining your positioning. Melee builds should Veil through orbs to destroy them and heal simultaneously.
Phase 2 70-30% Summons wolf packs (3 waves of 4-5 wolves each), dark AoE pools on the floor (persistent damage zones dealing ~8% HP/sec), enhanced sword combos (5-hit chain, faster animation, wider arc). Kill wolves immediately — they heal Dracula if they reach him, and the cumulative heal from an unchecked pack can undo minutes of progress. Switch targets the moment wolves spawn. Use AoE abilities (Q-spin for greatsword, rain-of-arrows + Raging Tempest for longbow) to clear wolves efficiently. Stay out of dark pools at all costs — they drain roughly 8% HP per second and Dracula will actively try to knock or push you into them. The visual cue is a dark purple circular glow on the floor; watch for the floor effect, not the particle trail. Punish windows are shorter in this phase; limit yourself to 1-2 hits per opening and do not overstay.
Phase 3 30-0% Blood shield (absorbs ~30% of his remaining HP as damage before breaking), faster attack speed on all abilities (roughly 40% faster animations), desperation mode — chains attacks with almost no recovery windows between them. Save your Ultimate (Heart Strike or Raging Tempest) and all remaining potions for this phase. Pop everything the moment the blood shield goes up — you need maximum burst to break through it before his accelerated attacks overwhelm you. Below 20% HP, dodge is life — prioritize dodging over dealing damage. Dracula will chain teleport strikes and sword combos with almost no gap. Wait for the teleport landing animation (the only guaranteed safe window in Phase 3), land exactly 1 hit, then dodge out. Patience wins this phase; greed wipes it. Remember: a dead vampire deals zero DPS.
Phase Transition Warning: At each threshold (70% and 30%), Dracula becomes briefly invulnerable and plays a transition animation. Do not waste cooldowns or potions during these transitions — your damage will read as zero and your cooldowns will be down when the real Phase 3 pressure begins. Use the 3-4 seconds to reposition to the center of the arena and top off your health with a Blood Rose Brew.

Common Mistakes & Recovery

1. Getting Greedy — Over-Committing on Punish Windows

This is the #1 cause of Dracula wipes, especially for greatsword players. You land 2 hits, see the HP bar move, and go for a third — and Dracula's follow-up slash catches you mid-swing. Discipline is everything. Two hits max per window in Phase 1 and Phase 2. One hit in Phase 3. Count them out loud if it helps. If you're unsure whether you have time for another swing, you don't. A dead DPS does zero DPS.

2. Ignoring Blood Orbs

Blood orbs move slowly and seem low-priority, which is exactly why they wipe runs. Each orb that reaches Dracula heals him for approximately 5% of his max HP. In a fight where you're slowly chipping him down over 8-12 minutes, letting even 3 orbs through effectively resets a minute of progress. Destroy orbs as soon as they spawn. Treat them as priority targets equal to the boss himself — the DPS you lose by switching to an orb is far less than the DPS you lose undoing a 5% heal.

3. Standing in Dark Pools

Phase 2 dark pools deal rapid percentage-based HP drain (~8%/sec). The visual can be hard to see amid the particle effects of spells, sword trails, and wolf models. Watch for the dark purple circular glow on the floor — not the particle trail above it. If your health starts ticking down rapidly and you cannot immediately identify why, you are standing in a pool. Move immediately, even if it means canceling a charged attack. The damage ticks are fast enough that a 2-second delay can cost you 15-20% of your HP.

4. Panic Dodging

Burning both dodge charges the moment Dracula looks at you leaves you defenseless for his actual attack. Watch his animations carefully: he telegraphs every major attack with a distinct wind-up. Side-step the sword combo with regular movement (no dodge needed). Save dodge for the teleport strike — he vanishes, and roughly 0.5 seconds later reappears behind you. That is your dodge timing. If running the Scholar build, save Frost Barrier for his charge attack. Dodging on reaction to the actual attack animation, not on fear, is the single biggest skill gap in this fight.

5. Not Killing Wolves in Phase 2

Wolves in Phase 2 do not deal threatening direct damage, which leads many players to ignore them and tunnel-vision Dracula. This is a trap. Wolves that reach Dracula heal him significantly — and 3 waves of unchecked wolves can heal him from 40% back to 65%, effectively resetting the entire phase. Switch to wolves the moment they spawn. AoE abilities (greatsword Q-spin, longbow rain-of-arrows, Raging Tempest) make quick work of them. Only return to Dracula when the last wolf in the current wave is dead. The 5 seconds you spend clearing adds is nothing compared to the 2+ minutes you lose if Dracula heals.

Recovery Tips

  • If you fall below 30% HP with no potions left, switch to pure survival mode. Dodge only, no attacking, until Blood Rose Brew comes off cooldown or Veil of Blood resets. Surviving is always more important than an extra 200 damage.
  • If Dracula heals significantly in Phase 2 due to missed orbs or wolves, do not panic or tilt. The mechanics do not change — you simply have to execute cleanly for longer. The fight is still winnable as long as you maintain discipline.
  • If you die in Phase 3 with Dracula below 10%, the run-back is absolutely worth it. Dracula does not reset above a phase threshold once he has passed it. Your progress is saved — get back in there.
  • If you die more than 5 times in a row, take a 10-minute break. Dracula is a mental endurance fight. Frustration leads to greedy plays, and greedy plays lead to more wipes.

Dracula is the hardest solo boss in V Rising, but with the right build and disciplined execution, he is absolutely beatable. Greatsword Brute if you trust your timing and want maximum burst. Longbow Scholar if you want consistency and a more forgiving learning curve. Either way, the fight rewards patience over aggression — the vampire who dodges first and attacks second is the one who walks out of that throne room alive.
