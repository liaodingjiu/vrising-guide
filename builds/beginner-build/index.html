Follow this table from fresh spawn to endgame. Each stage tells you exactly what weapon, blood type, and armor to use — and which boss unlocks the next upgrade.

🦴 Early Game Setup (GS 0-30)

Your first 2-3 hours in Vardoran. Survival is the priority — this build keeps you alive while you learn the basics.

GS 0-15: The Bone Phase

SlotChoiceHow to Get
WeaponBone SwordCraft from bones dropped by skeletons in the starter graveyard
BloodBrute (any quality)Kill any brute-type enemy. Bandit Thugs and Bears count as Brute blood.
ArmorBoneguard SetCraft from bones + animal hide. Your first full set.
SpellShadowbolt (starter)Your only spell at this stage. Use it to pull enemies one at a time.
First Hour Priority: Don't fight everything. Gather bones, plant fibers, and animal hide. Craft Boneguard armor and a Bone Sword. Find a Brute-blood enemy (bears are easiest) and feed. Then kill Alpha Wolf — the first V Blood boss — to unlock Leatherworking.

GS 15-30: The Copper Phase

WeaponCopper SwordMine copper ore in Bandit Copper Mine (Farbane Woods). Smelt into bars.
BloodBrute 20%+ qualityStart checking blood quality. 20%+ is common on bears and bandit leaders.
ArmorNightstalker SetCopper bars + leather. Your first armor with a damage bonus (+physical).
SpellsChaos Volley + Veil of BloodChaos Volley from Lidia the Chaos Archer. Veil of Blood from any high-level blood carrier.

This is where you graduate from Sword to Greatsword and start fighting real bosses. The training wheels come off.

GS 30-50: The Greatsword Switch

BloodBrute 40%+ qualityFarm bears in Dunley Farmlands. 40%+ Brute blood gives noticeable attack speed.

GS 50-70: Dark Silver Power Spike

SlotChoiceReason
WeaponDark Silver GreatswordSignificant damage jump. Kill Octavian for Dark Silver smithing.
BloodBrute 60%+ qualityAt 60%+, the attack speed bonus is transformative. Greatsword no longer feels slow.
ArmorDawnthorn SetHighest +Health bonus of this tier. Gives you the tankiness to trade hits with bosses.

Don't Skip Brute Blood Farming: Many beginners ignore blood quality and struggle. A 60%+ Brute blood transforms Greatsword from "too slow" to "devastating." Spend 30 minutes farming bears in Dunley — it's worth it. Use the Blood Hunger system (default key: V) to check blood quality before feeding.

⚠️ Common Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

These five mistakes kill more new players than any boss. Avoid them and you'll progress twice as fast.

Mistake #1: Ignoring Blood Quality. Any blood type at any quality gives some benefit, but low-quality blood (under 20%) gives almost nothing. A 60%+ Brute blood doubles your damage output through attack speed alone. Always check blood quality (press V) before feeding.
Mistake #2: Skipping Armor Upgrades. Gear Score gates boss fights — if your GS is below the boss's level, you deal reduced damage and take extra. Upgrade your armor before fighting each tier of boss. A full set of Hollowfang at GS 30 is worth more than a fancy weapon with Boneguard armor.
Mistake #3: Fighting Everything. Early game enemies drop almost nothing of value. Run past non-essential mobs and focus on V Blood bosses. Each boss unlocks a crafting recipe or ability — they are the only mandatory fights.
Mistake #4: Using the Wrong Weapon Type. Sword is the only beginner-friendly weapon. Reaper and Greatsword are better but require experience to use effectively. If you're dying a lot, switch back to Sword — its faster attack speed gives you more dodge windows.
Mistake #5: Neglecting Castle Upgrades. Your castle is your progression engine. Upgrade Castle Heart → Workshop → Forge → Tailor Bench in that order. Every gear upgrade requires the corresponding crafting station at the right level.

🎓 When to Graduate from Beginner Build

The beginner build is a stepping stone. Know when it's time to move on to a specialized build.

MilestoneWhat ChangesNext Build
Reach GS 70You have Bloodmoon armor and a solid Greatsword. Brute blood at 60%+.Full Greatsword PvE Build
Kill your first endgame boss (Styx, Dracula)You understand boss patterns and can survive melee range.Boss Killer Build
Start farming resources efficientlyYou need large quantities of materials for endgame crafting.Reaper Scholar PvE Build (#1)
Want to play PvPPvP is a completely different game. PvE builds don't work against players.Solo Build (PvP variant)

The beginner build is designed to get you to GS 70 safely. After that, the game opens up and you can choose your specialization. Most players branch into Greatsword Brute (bosses) or Reaper Scholar (farming) by GS 70.
