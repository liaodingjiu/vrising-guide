🧙 Stationary Caster Bosses

These bosses stand still to cast spells, giving you long damage windows. The key is dodging their projectiles and punishing their cast animations.

Best Build: Greatsword Brute

SlotChoiceReason
WeaponGreatswordCasters have long recovery animations. One fully charged heavy during their cast = 30% of their HP.
BloodBrute 90%+Attack speed lets you land charged heavies faster before the next spell comes out.
SpellsVeil of Blood + Sanguine Coil + Chaos VolleyStandard melee sustain. Coil heals through their DoT effects.

Strategy

  1. Stay at mid-range to bait their spell cast.
  2. Dodge the projectile (side-step, don't dash — save dash for gap closing).
  3. E (Lunge) during their recovery animation → charged heavy → Q → auto-attack until next cast.
  4. If they teleport, Veil of Blood to chase, then restart the cycle.
Key Insight: Caster bosses have a "tell" before every spell — a visual effect or sound cue. Learn the tell, pre-dodge, and you'll never get hit. Lidia raises her bow, Vincent raises his staff, Azariel glows gold. Watch for the tell, not the projectile.

⚔️ Aggressive Melee Bosses

These bosses are in your face constantly. Fast combos, gap closers, and minimal recovery windows. You need a weapon that can trade hits and a blood type that shreds their defense.

Best Build: Spear Warrior

SlotChoiceReason
WeaponSpearLonger reach than Greatsword — you can poke melee bosses from outside their swing range. Q (Thousand Spears) applies multi-hit pressure.
BloodWarrior 60%+Defense reduction on hit. Melee bosses have high armor — Warrior blood strips it, amplifying every subsequent hit.
SpellsVeil of Blood + Frost Barrier + Sanguine CoilFrost Barrier absorbs their combo while you attack through it. Swap Chaos Volley for Barrier.

Strategy

  1. Let them engage first. Melee bosses always open with a gap closer — dodge it.
  2. Pop Frost Barrier after their opener. They'll continue attacking into your barrier while you deal damage.
  3. Poke with Spear thrusts (auto-attack range is longer than theirs). Maintain spacing.
  4. When they enter their multi-hit combo, dash behind them with Veil of Blood and Q-spin.
  5. Repeat — let them engage, barrier, poke, dash-behind-punish. Patience beats aggression against melee bosses.
Octavian Specific: Octavian is the ultimate melee boss test. His spin attack hits everything around him — do NOT be near him when he starts spinning. Spear's range lets you poke him between spins. Warrior blood strips his high physical defense. If you can solo Octavian with this build, every other melee boss is easier.

💥 Heavy AoE Bosses

These bosses fill the arena with fire, poison, or projectiles. Melee uptime is near zero — you need range and mobility to survive.

Best Build: Longbow Scholar

SlotChoiceReason
WeaponLongbowMaximum range. You can damage these bosses from outside their AoE zones entirely.
BloodScholar 90%+Cooldown reset keeps your dodge and spells available. More dodges = more survival.
SpellsVeil of Frost + Chaos Volley + Sanguine CoilFrost trail slows bosses as they chase you through AoE zones. Coil for emergency heals.

Strategy

  1. Circle the arena clockwise. AoE bosses place ground effects in a pattern — moving in one direction avoids overlap.
  2. Charge an arrow while moving (you can walk while charging Longbow). Release when boss pauses to cast.
  3. Veil of Frost through the boss when cornered — the ice trail forces them to walk through slow to reach you.
  4. Never greed for extra shots. AoE bosses punish stationary targets. One charged arrow → move → one charged arrow → move.
Solarus Specific: Solarus fills the arena with holy fire. Circle the edge of the room — the fire is densest in the center. Longbow charged arrows from the perimeter clear this fight safely. Scholar blood keeps your Veil of Frost dash available for when fire blocks your path.

🔄 Phase-Transition Bosses

The hardest boss type. At 50% and 25% HP, they change attack patterns and spawn adds. You need AoE clear for adds AND single-target for the boss.

Best Build: Reaper Scholar

SlotChoiceReason
WeaponReaperE (Tendon Swing) clears adds in one spin. Q (Howling Harvest) hits the boss AND any adds behind them. Best weapon for multi-target boss fights.
BloodScholar 90%+Hitting adds accelerates cooldown resets. More resets = more E-spins = adds never pile up.
SpellsVeil of Chaos + Chaos Volley + Sanguine CoilClone recasts let you double-volley the boss while E-spin handles adds.

Strategy

  1. Phase 1 (100%-50%): Learn the initial pattern. Play conservatively — save your ultimate for phase 2.
  2. Phase Transition (50%): Boss becomes invulnerable briefly, adds spawn. Immediately E-spin into the add cluster → Q through everything. Clear adds in under 10 seconds.
  3. Phase 2 (50%-25%): New attack patterns. Be cautious for the first 30 seconds while you learn the new moves.
  4. Phase Transition (25%): Same as before — adds spawn, E-spin clears them. Boss is vulnerable again after adds die.
  5. Phase 3 (25%-0%): Most dangerous phase. Use Heart Strike as soon as boss drops below 25% — the heal covers the increased damage output.
Dracula Specific: Dracula has 3 phases with add spawns at 66% and 33%. Reaper Scholar is the only build that can handle his adds AND maintain boss DPS simultaneously. At 33%, Dracula's final phase introduces a blood pool attack that covers half the arena — save Veil of Chaos for crossing the pool safely.
