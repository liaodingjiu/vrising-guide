V Rising Greatsword Build

The Greatsword is V Rising's premier burst-damage weapon — slow but devastating. A fully charged Greatsword heavy attack deals the highest single-hit damage in the game, making it the weapon of choice for boss killing, PvP raiding, and any situation where you need to delete a target in one rotation. This build guide shows you exactly how to maximize that power with the right blood type, armor, spells, and jewels. Every recommendation tested through multiple full playthroughs on Brutal difficulty.

⚔️ Why Greatsword?

The Greatsword rewards patience and timing. It's not the fastest weapon, but it hits harder than anything else in Vardoran.

StrengthDetail
Highest single-hit damageCharged heavy attack deals 250-400% weapon damage depending on charge level
Wide AoE Q skillSpin Slash hits all enemies in a 360° radius — excellent for clearing adds during boss fights
Gap closer built inE skill (Lunge) closes distance and applies a slow — pairs perfectly with charged heavy
Tanky by natureHigher base defense multiplier than lighter weapons — you can trade hits and win
Devastating in PvPOne full combo (E → Q → charged heavy) can 100-to-0 a squishy target

Tradeoffs

  • Slow swing speed — you're vulnerable during charged attacks. Brute blood is mandatory for this reason.
  • Predictable — skilled PvP opponents will dodge your charged heavy. You need to set it up with CC or bait their dodge first.
  • Poor at multi-target sustained DPS — Reaper or Longbow is better for farming packs of enemies.

⚔️ PvE Greatsword Build

This build maximizes single-target burst damage for boss killing while maintaining enough AoE for add clearing.

Full PvE Configuration

SlotChoiceReason
WeaponGreatsword (Sanguine tier)Highest raw damage. Legendary variant from General's Greatsword is best-in-slot.
Blood TypeBrute 60%+ qualityAttack speed steroid. At 90%+ quality, attack speed boost reaches 25% — transforms Greatsword's feel.
ArmorSanguine Armor SetMaximum physical damage bonus + health pool. Bloodmoon set at GS 70-91 as stepping stone.
Travel SpellVeil of BloodDash through enemies + heal on contact. Closes gaps and sustains in prolonged boss fights.
Basic Spell 1Sanguine CoilHeal on hit — you're in melee taking damage, this keeps you alive without potions.
Basic Spell 2Chaos VolleyRanged option for pulling bosses and dealing damage when mechanics force you to disengage.
UltimateHeart StrikeMassive single-target burst that heals you for damage dealt. Combined with charged heavy + Q, this is your boss-killing combo.

PvE Combat Rotation

  1. Opener: Chaos Volley (pull boss) → E (Lunge) close gap → Q (Spin Slash) for initial burst
  2. Damage Phase: Auto-attack x2 → charge heavy → dodge boss mechanic → repeat
  3. Burst Window: When boss is staggered/CC'd: E → Q → fully charged heavy → Heart Strike. This sequence deletes 20-30% of a boss HP bar in one window.
  4. Sustain: Veil of Blood through boss when low HP → Sanguine Coil after dodge → back to damage rotation
Boss Tip: Greatsword's charged heavy has hyper armor during the final frames of the animation — you won't be interrupted by damage. Time your charge to finish right as the boss's attack lands. You'll eat the hit but trade 400% weapon damage for it. With Brute's health bonus, you win every trade against non-boss enemies.

🗡️ PvP Greatsword Build

In PvP, Greatsword is a punishing weapon — one mistake from your opponent and they lose half their HP. The key is creating those mistakes.

Full PvP Configuration

SlotChoiceReason
WeaponGreatsword (Sanguine tier)Same as PvE — raw damage is king in PvP. Legendary General's Greatsword for the +physical damage affix.
Blood TypeBrute 60%+ qualityAttack speed is even more important in PvP — you need to land your charged heavy before they dodge.
ArmorDawnthorn Set (GS 50-70) → SanguineDawnthorn gives +health bonus which is more valuable in PvP than Bloodmoon's +physical damage. Survive their burst, then one-shot them.
Travel SpellVeil of ChaosIllusion dash leaves a clone that recasts your next spell. Double Chaos Volley = 2x the ranged pressure.
Basic Spell 1Frost BarrierAbsorbs damage and slows attackers. Pop this before charging a heavy — they either eat the heavy or waste their dodge escaping the slow.
Basic Spell 2Chaos VolleyRanged poke. Land both projectiles for 15-20% of a player's HP before the melee engagement even starts.
UltimateHeart StrikeYour finisher. Use after they've burned their dodge and you've applied CC. A point-blank Heart Strike on a defenseless target is a kill confirm.

PvP Engagement Flow

  1. Poke Phase: Chaos Volley at range. Veil of Chaos → recast Volley. Force them to approach you.
  2. Setup: When they close in, pop Frost Barrier. They can't trade into the barrier + your charged heavy.
  3. Catch: E (Lunge) when they try to disengage. The slow from Lunge guarantees a partially charged heavy.
  4. Kill Confirm: Enemy below 40% HP, dodge burned, no barrier → E + Q + fully charged heavy + Heart Strike. Dead.
PvP Mind Game: Start charging a heavy attack, then cancel it with a dodge. 80% of opponents will burn their dodge reacting to the "incoming heavy." Now they have no dodge, and your actual heavy is still ready to use. The Greatsword is a psychological weapon — the threat of the charged heavy is often more valuable than the attack itself.

🩸 Best Blood Type for Greatsword

Blood type makes or breaks a Greatsword build. Here's the definitive ranking.

TierBlood TypeQuality NeededSynergy
SBrute60%+ (ideally 90%+)Attack speed steroid directly compensates for Greatsword's slow swings. +Health bonus makes you tanky enough to trade hits. At 90%+ quality, 25% attack speed transforms the weapon entirely.
AScholar60%+Cooldown reset on hit means more Q-spins and more E-lunges. Better for PvE where you can consistently land 8 hits. Less effective in PvP where hit consistency is lower.
BRogue90%+Armor expose amplifies Greatsword burst, but Rogue lacks the attack speed or tankiness Greatsword needs. Viable only in coordinated PvP with a CC partner.
CWarrior60%+Defense reduction helps sustained damage, but Greatsword plays in bursts, not sustained DPS. Outclassed by Brute for this weapon.

✨ Best Spells for Greatsword

Spells fill the gaps in Greatsword's kit — mobility, sustain, and ranged pressure.

Must-Have: A Gap Closer

Greatsword's biggest weakness is getting kited. You must have a movement spell. Veil of Blood (heal + dash through enemies) is the best all-around choice. Veil of Chaos (clone + recast) is better for PvP. Veil of Frost (slow trail) is situationally useful for controlling chokepoints.

PvE Spell Combo: Sustain Build

  • Veil of Blood — dash through boss, heal, position behind for charged heavy
  • Sanguine Coil — ranged heal on hit, use between boss mechanics
  • Chaos Volley — ranged damage for when boss forces disengage
  • Heart Strike (Ult) — massive burst + heal finisher

PvP Spell Combo: Catch & Kill

  • Veil of Chaos — double cast Chaos Volley through clone for surprise burst
  • Frost Barrier — absorb return damage while you charge heavy
  • Chaos Volley — ranged pressure, force them to engage on your terms
  • Heart Strike (Ult) — finisher on CC'd target without dodge

🎯 Playstyle & Combat Tips

Master these techniques to get the most out of your Greatsword.

Cancel your heavies. Charged heavy → dodge cancel is your most important technique. It baits enemy dodges, lets you reposition, and keeps opponents guessing. A Greatsword player who never cancels heavies is predictable; one who cancels 50% of the time is terrifying.
E is not just a gap closer. Lunge applies a 30% slow for 2 seconds. Use E → immediate charged heavy — the slow guarantees at least a partial charge lands even if they dodge.
Q-spin through enemies, not at them. Spin Slash deals damage in a circle around you. Dash into a pack of enemies first (Veil of Blood), then Q in the center. You'll hit everything instead of just the front row.
Know your matchups. Greatsword destroys other slow weapons (Mace, Spear) and mages (Crossbow, Longbow if you close the gap). It struggles against Claws and Slashers — fast weapons that can poke and disengage before your swing finishes. Against fast weapons, play defensively and punish their mistakes rather than forcing engages.

❓ Greatsword FAQ

Where do I get the best Greatsword?

The General's Reaper (misleading name — it's a Greatsword) drops from General Valencia the Depraved in Gloomrot. It has the highest base physical damage of any Greatsword. For endgame, craft the Ancestral Greatsword at the Ancestral Forge for customizable legendary affixes. See Boss Kill Order for Valencia's location →

Can I use Greatsword from the start of the game?

Yes — you can craft a Copper Greatsword as early as GS 15. However, Greatsword feels best at GS 50+ when you have enough attack speed (Brute blood + gear) to offset the slow swing speed. Before GS 50, consider using Sword or Spear which are more forgiving while you learn boss mechanics.

Is Greatsword better than Slashers for PvP?

Different roles. Greatsword is a punisher — you win by capitalizing on enemy mistakes with massive burst. Slashers are pressure weapons — you win by constantly harassing with stealth openers and bleeds. Greatsword beats Slashers if you can land the E → charged heavy combo. Slashers beat Greatsword if they never let you land it. The matchup is roughly 50/50 and comes down to player skill.
