🏆 #1 Reaper Scholar — Best Overall PvE Build

Reaper is the PvE king. Its wide sweeping attacks clear packs of enemies faster than any weapon, and Scholar blood means your spells have near-zero cooldowns.

Full Configuration

SlotChoiceReason
WeaponReaper (Sanguine tier)360-degree AoE E skill clears entire rooms. Q (Howling Harvest) throws a returning projectile that hits twice. Best farming weapon in the game.
Blood TypeScholar 90%+ qualityCooldown reset on every 8th hit. With Reaper hitting 5+ enemies per swing, you reset spells every 2-3 seconds. Infinite spellcasting loop.
ArmorSanguine Armor SetMaximum physical + spell power. Reaper scales with both — your weapon skills are physical, your spells are magical.
Travel SpellVeil of ChaosDash leaves a clone that recasts your next spell. Double Chaos Volley or double Sanguine Coil for free.

UltimateHeart StrikeMassive single-target burst that heals you. Use when boss is below 50% HP for a clean finish.

Why Reaper Wins PvE

Reaper's E skill (Tendon Swing) is the best AoE clear in the game — a 360-degree spin that hits every enemy around you and knocks them back. In a room with 10+ enemies, each swing generates 10+ hits. Scholar blood counts every hit toward the 8-hit cooldown reset. The result: you can cast Chaos Volley, E-spin, and by the time the spin finishes your spells are already off cooldown. This loop makes Reaper Scholar the fastest farming build and one of the safest boss-killing builds.

Pro Tip: Reaper's E → Q combo is your bread and butter. E into a pack of enemies (knocks them all back into a tight group), then immediately Q. The returning projectile from Q will hit everything in that packed group for double damage. With Scholar blood, this combo is available every 5 seconds.

🛡️ #3 Summoner Scholar — Safest Build

The Summoner build lets minions do the work while you stay safe. Ideal for players learning boss mechanics or tackling Brutal difficulty for the first time.

Full Configuration

SlotChoiceReason
WeaponLongbow (Sanguine tier)Ranged attacks keep you at safe distance. Charged arrow pierces through minions into the boss.
Blood TypeScholar 60%+ qualityCooldown reset = more summons. Your minions generate hits for Scholar procs.
ArmorSanguine Armor SetSpell power scaling for your summons. Summon damage scales with spell power.
Travel SpellVeil of FrostDash leaves a slowing trail. Kite the boss through it while minions attack.
Basic Spell 1Summon SkeletonDistraction + free DPS. Bosses often target skeletons, giving you free damage windows.
Basic Spell 2Death KnightTanky summon that draws aggro. Combined with Skeleton, you have 2-3 bodies between you and the boss.
UltimateArmy of the DeadSummons 4 skeletons. With Scholar resets, you can maintain near-100% uptime on your army in long fights.
Safety Strategy: The key to Summoner is positioning. Summon minions, let them draw aggro, then circle behind the boss for backstab damage with Longbow. If minions die, dash away and re-summon. You can kill every boss in the game without taking a single hit with this build — it's slow, but it's the safest approach in V Rising.

📊 Build Comparison & When to Use Each

Not sure which build to pick? Match your playstyle to the right build.

Your PlaystyleRecommended BuildWhy
"I want the fastest clears"Reaper Scholar (#1)Unmatched AoE farming speed. Best for resource runs and mob-heavy boss fights.
"I want to melt bosses fast"Greatsword Brute (#2)Highest single-target burst. Ideal for V Blood hunting and endgame boss farming.
"I'm new and want safety"Summoner Scholar (#3)Minions tank for you. Learn boss patterns without risking death.
"I prefer ranged combat"Longbow Scholar (#4)Kite from maximum range. Best for players who dislike melee.
"I want one do-it-all build"Spear Warrior (#5)Decent at everything, master of none. No weapon swapping needed.

Most endgame players keep two weapons hotkeyed: Reaper for farming and Greatsword for bosses. You don't have to commit to just one build — V Rising encourages weapon swapping based on the situation.
