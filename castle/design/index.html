🏭 Example 1: The Riverside Forge (PvE, Mid-Game)

Functional crafting fortress — efficient without feeling like a factory.

AspectDetail
LocationRiverside plot, Dunley Farmlands — flat terrain, water, central map
PlotMedium (150-200 tiles)
ThemeIndustrial vampire forge — stone walls, workshop floors, visible forge room
Features4 crafting stations in cross pattern around heart, riverside garden, servant quarters with river view
Best ForMid-game players focused on progression. Every station 2 tiles from storage.

🏔️ Example 2: The Mountain Keep (PvP, Endgame)

Nearly unraidable fortress — terrain does half the defending.

AspectDetail
LocationCliffside plot, Silverlight Hills — one accessible side, 3 protected by terrain
PlotLarge (200-250 tiles)
ThemeImpenetrable fortress — dark stone, iron fences, torch-lit corridors
FeaturesSingle chokepoint entrance, 3-layer honeycomb, servant patrols covering approach, heart buried in mountain face, multiple false doors
Best ForPvP servers. Full raid costs 15-20 Major Explosive Boxes.

🌿 Example 3: The Hidden Villa (Solo PvP)

Camouflaged base — relies on not being found.

AspectDetail
LocationForest-edge, Farbane Woods — hidden by trees, off roads
PlotSmall (50-100 tiles)
ThemeAbandoned villa — ivy walls, overgrown garden, no visible torches
FeaturesNo exterior lighting, natural exterior blends with terrain, compact but complete, backup coffin in secondary base
Best ForSolo PvP — raiders can't raid what they can't find.

✨ Aesthetic Design Tips

Lighting is atmosphere. Torches near entrance (welcoming), blue braziers in prison (cold), red torches in blood altar (vampiric). Never put torches on outer PvP walls.
Use height variation. Multi-story feels grand. Heart room = highest ceiling. Staircases in corners save floor tiles.
Theme your rooms. Different floors/walls per room type. Workshop floor for crafting, Crypt for servants. Material contrast.
Leave negative space. 2-tile corridors, small courtyard, empty balcony. A packed castle feels claustrophobic.
