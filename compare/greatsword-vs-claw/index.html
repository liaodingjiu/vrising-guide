⚔️🦅 Head-to-Head: GS vs Claws

Claws beat Greatsword 65% of the time. GS needs E→charged heavy. Claws dictate every engagement with Pounce range advantage. At equal skill, Claws control tempo = fewer mistakes. → Matchup guide for all weapons

📈 Skill Requirements

Key: Greatsword better first 50 hours. Claws better after 50 hours. Crossover = when you can predict opponents.

✅ The Verdict

Choose Greatsword if: New (<50hrs), PvE focused, prefer patience, want one build for everything.

Choose Claws if: 50+ hours, PvP focused, prefer aggression, willing to die learning.

Use Both (real answer): Most endgame players max both. GS for PvE bosses + raids. Claws for PvP duels.
