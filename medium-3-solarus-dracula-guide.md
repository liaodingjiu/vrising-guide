# Stuck on Solarus and Dracula? Here's What I Learned After 200+ Attempts

## Quick Summary
After 1,200+ hours and every boss killed on Brutal, the two that took the most attempts weren't what I expected. Solarus's listed Gear Score is a lie. Dracula has a hidden phase nobody warns you about. Here's everything I wish I knew before walking into those arenas.

---

## The Two Walls of V Rising

Most bosses in V Rising fall after 3–5 attempts once you learn their patterns. Then you hit Solarus and Dracula — and suddenly you're dying double-digit times to mechanics the game never explains.

I burned 30+ attempts on Dracula alone. Eleven on Solarus. I watched every YouTube guide. I read every Reddit thread. And most of them skipped the details that actually matter.

Here's what those guides missed.

---

## Solarus the Immaculate: The GS 68 Lie

The Blood Altar lists Solarus at Gear Score 68. This is technically true and practically useless.

Solarus deals **holy damage** — the rarest damage type in V Rising, and the one most players have zero resistance to. He self-heals. He summons adds that heal him. The arena is packed with silver-wielding mobs before you even reach him.

**Realistic minimum: GS 75+.** Don't walk in at 68. You'll regret it.

**The Phase 3 Trick Nobody Told Me**

At roughly 30% HP, Solarus enters Phase 3. He channels a massive AoE that APPEARS to cover the entire arena. Eleven deaths before I figured it out: **the pillar shadows block it.** Stand behind a pillar. That's the entire mechanic.

Eleven deaths. One pillar. Nobody mentioned it in any guide I watched.

**The Prep That Finally Worked:**

- Scholar 90%+ blood (cooldown reset + spell crit)
- **Holy Resistance Potion** — 35% holy damage reduction. Non-negotiable.
- Kill Paladin adds immediately — they heal him
- One mobility spell + one defensive spell — not all offense
- Clear the silver mobs from the approach path before starting the fight

---

## Dracula the Immortal King: The Hidden Phase 4

Dracula is the final boss. GS 91. Four phases. You've been warned about the first three.

**Nobody warns you about Phase 4.** Because on Normal difficulty, it doesn't exist. Phase 4 is Brutal-only — and the Blood Altar doesn't mention it. You won't know it exists until you hit 25% HP on Brutal difficulty and the arena suddenly shrinks to a tiny circle with Dracula at the center.

In Phase 4, Dracula channels a blood storm that blocks most of the arena. The safe zone gets smaller as time goes on. He spams sword rain with expanded damage areas. He fires blood bolt walls every few seconds. He releases shockwaves to knock you into the blood storm — and the blood storm **hurts.**

The mistake that cost me 30+ attempts: **burning all my potions in Phase 3.** You need 10+ potions saved for Phase 4. If you run out, you're dead. No recovery. No second chance.

**The Prep That Finally Worked:**

- Scholar 90%+ blood
- **30+ healing potions** — craft them before you enter Mortium
- Save potions for Phase 4 — Phase 1–3 are survivable with life leech and defensive spells
- Bring a partner if you can — Dracula's aggro table bounces between players. Solo Dracula is genuinely the hardest achievement in the game
- Use the Blood Altar's Dracula tracking to practice the Phase 1–3 patterns until they're muscle memory

---

## The Universal Rules for Both

**1. Gear score is a floor, not a recommendation.** Both fights demand GS well above the listed number unless you're a mechanical god.

**2. Potions are not optional.** If you walk into either fight without healing potions, you're volunteering for a death walk.

**3. Scholar 90%+ blood makes both fights significantly easier.** The cooldown reset changes everything — it doubles your spell output.

**4. One mobility spell + one defensive spell.** All-offense builds get punished hard in both fights. You need escapes.

**5. Watch the full fight on YouTube before attempting.** Knowing the phase triggers and attack patterns in advance saves you from learning them through death.

---

## The Boss Order Matters Too

If you're stuck on these two, ask yourself: did you skip any bosses?

Solarus requires Holy Resistance gear, which means you should have cleared Azariel and Meredith first. Dracula requires the Blood Key, which means Solarus is mandatory. Some players try to sequence-break and end up fighting a boss without the counter-tools they should have picked up earlier.
