🧭 Quick Decision Guide: Farm or Craft?

If you read nothing else, read this. The single most important Whetstone decision in V Rising.

Need Whetstones?

↓

Do you have a Grinder built?

↓

NO → Farm bandit camps. Western Farbane loop: 3 camps, ~8 minutes, 3-5 Whetstones.

YES → Craft them. 1 Copper Ingot + 1 Stone Dust = 1 Whetstone at the Grinder. Always.

↓

Need only 1-2 urgently? Clear nearest bandit camp (2 min).

Need 10+ for weapon upgrades? Unlock Grinder immediately. Craft passively.

TL;DR — 3 rules to live by:
✓ Farm bandit camps for your first 10-15 Whetstones before GS 37.
✓ Unlock the Grinder (defeat Quincey, GS 37) and never farm again.
✓ Save excess Whetstones for Merciless Copper and Iron weapon upgrades.
✓ Never run a dedicated "Whetstone farming route" past mid-game — it's always less efficient than crafting.

🔍 Every Way to Get Whetstones

Four methods, ranked by when you'll use them — not by theoretical efficiency.

1. Loot — Bandit Camps (Early Game Only)

Availability: From GS 0. Bandit enemies and camp chests in Farbane Woods drop Whetstones at roughly 15-25% per enemy, with chests guaranteeing 1-2.

Efficiency: Moderate. A dedicated 8-minute route through 3 camps yields 3-5 Whetstones. Viable for your first 10-15 Whetstones. Inefficient past GS 30.

Recommended stage: GS 0-30. Stop once you have the Grinder.

Limitations: RNG-dependent — some runs yield zero. Bandit camps are also farmed by other players on multiplayer servers.

2. Craft — Grinder (Mid Game Onward)

Availability: From GS 37 (defeat Quincey the Bandit King → build Grinder workstation).

Recipe: 1 Copper Ingot (20 Copper Ore) + 1 Stone Dust (grind raw Stone at the same Grinder) = 1 Whetstone. Crafting time: ~5 seconds per unit.

Efficiency: High. Copper Ore and Stone are the two most abundant resources in the game. You accumulate them passively while doing anything involving mining or stone-clearing.

Recommended stage: GS 37-91. This is your permanent solution. Never farm Whetstones again after unlocking this.

Limitations: Requires a Grinder (1 workstation slot). Copper Ore must be smelted into Copper Ingots first at a Furnace.

3. Containers — Crates & Barrels (Supplementary Only)

Availability: From GS 0. Crates, barrels, and destructible objects in bandit camps occasionally contain Whetstones.

Efficiency: Low. Container loot is supplementary — you'll get a few while clearing camps but should never target containers specifically for Whetstones.

Recommended stage: N/A. Treat these as bonus drops, not a farming method.

Limitations: Extremely inconsistent. Containers have a large, diluted loot pool.

4. Enemy Drops — Bandit-Specific (Early Game)

Availability: From GS 0. Bandit enemies (all variants: Bandit Archer, Bandit Soldier, Bandit Brute) have a chance to drop Whetstones on death.

Efficiency: Decent for early game. Bandits are plentiful in Farbane Woods and respawn every few minutes. Combined with camp chests and Copper Ore nodes in the same areas, this creates an efficient multi-purpose farming loop.

Recommended stage: GS 0-30. Transition to crafting as soon as possible.

Limitations: Drop rate is not officially documented — estimated at 15-25% based on community testing. Higher-level bandits (Dunley Farmlands) do not have significantly better drop rates, making Farbane Woods the best farming zone regardless of progression.

🌱 Early Game Strategy (GS 0-30)

Your first 10-15 Whetstones set the foundation for the next 30 hours of progression.

Your first objective: get 10-12 Whetstones to craft your first Merciless Copper weapon. At this stage, you don't have the Grinder — farming bandit camps is your only option.

The Western Farbane Loop (Optimal Early Route)

  1. Start: Farbane Woods Waygate.
  2. Stop 1: Western cliff bandit camp — mine all Copper nodes along the cliff face, clear the camp (bandits + chest).
  3. Stop 2: Ancient Village (northwest) — larger camp, more bandits, multiple chests. Higher yield but more enemies.
  4. Stop 3: Bandit Camp Overlook (north) — smaller camp, good for finishing off a run.
  5. Return: Waygate back, or walk if carrying Copper Ore (waygates block teleportation with ore by default).

Yield per loop: 3-5 Whetstones, 60-80 Copper Ore, 8-12 Gem Stones. Time: ~8 minutes. Repeat 2-3 times to fully gear your first Merciless Copper weapon.

Why this route works: It combines three objectives into one loop — Whetstone farming, Copper Ore mining, and Gem Stone collection. The Copper and Gem Stones you collect will be used immediately after you build the Grinder. You're not just farming Whetstones — you're stockpiling for the mid-game transition.

🔄 Mid Game Transition: Farm → Craft

This is the section missing from almost every other Whetstone guide. The moment your entire resource strategy changes.

The turning point is GS 37 — defeating Quincey the Bandit King in Dunley Farmlands. Quincey unlocks the Grinder workstation, which unlocks the Whetstone crafting recipe. From this moment forward, you should never run a dedicated Whetstone farming route again.

Here's why the math changes completely at GS 37:

  • Before GS 37: 8-minute bandit loop = 3-5 Whetstones. You have no alternative.
  • After GS 37: 8-minute Copper mining loop = 60-80 Copper Ore = 3-4 Copper Ingots = 3-4 Whetstones. Plus the Copper Ingots you don't spend on Whetstones are available for weapons and armor. The bandit loop's Copper is also available, but mining-focused routes yield far more ore per minute.

The key insight: crafting Whetstones has no opportunity cost. Copper Ore and Stone are collected passively during all other activities — boss runs, exploration, building. Every time you return to base with a full inventory, you dump Copper Ore into the Furnace and Stone into the Grinder. Whetstones appear in your storage without a single dedicated farming minute.

The transition checklist:
✓ Defeat Quincey the Bandit King (GS 37).
✓ Build the Grinder workstation.
✓ Smelt Copper Ore → Copper Ingots at Furnace.
✓ Grind raw Stone → Stone Dust at Grinder.
✓ Craft Whetstones: 1 Copper Ingot + 1 Stone Dust.
✓ Stop running bandit camp loops for Whetstones. Forever.

📦 Long-Term Resource Strategy

Once crafting is established, Whetstone management becomes about efficiency and planning — not acquisition.

Inventory Management

Keep a dedicated chest near your Grinder for Whetstone ingredients: one slot for Copper Ingots, one slot for Stone Dust. When both hit 10+, craft a batch. This prevents the "I have Copper but no Stone Dust" bottleneck that happens when you craft one at a time.

Crafting Priority

Whetstones compete with weapon and armor crafting for Copper Ingots. Priority order: weapon upgrades > armor upgrades > Whetstone stockpile > excess. Always ensure you have 3-5 Whetstones in reserve before spending Copper Ingots on non-essential crafts.

Storage Recommendations

Maintain a minimum stock of 10 Whetstones at all times from mid-game onward. This covers unexpected upgrade opportunities (new weapon blueprint drops) and emergency crafting. Excess Whetstones beyond 20 can safely be spent on Grinder recipes like Gem Dust production.

Avoid Over-Farming

The most common resource mistake in V Rising is over-farming low-tier materials. A stockpile of 50 Whetstones in the late game is wasted inventory space and wasted Copper that could have been Dark Silver or Sanguine-tier components. Whetstones are a Tier 2 material — their relevance peaks at GS 30-50 and declines steadily after Iron-tier weapons are crafted.

Opportunity Cost

Every minute spent actively farming Whetstones is a minute not spent killing bosses, progressing the main quest, or farming higher-tier materials. The Grinder turns Whetstone acquisition into a background process — the most efficient strategy is to let it run while you do more important things.

⚖️ Craft or Farm? The Decision Framework

This is the core differentiator of this guide. Most guides just list methods — here's how to choose between them.

Category Craft (Grinder) Farm (Bandit Camps)
Speed (per Whetstone) ~5 seconds + passive ore collection ~2 minutes per Whetstone (RNG)
Reliability 100% — guaranteed output ~15-25% per bandit — RNG-heavy
Resource Cost 1 Copper Ingot + 1 Stone Dust Time + weapon durability
Time Cost Near zero — passive crafting ~8 min per 3-5 Whetstones
Best Stage GS 37-91 (mid to endgame) GS 0-37 (early game only)
Recommendation Always — once unlocked Only for first 10-15 Whetstones

The trade-off is not "which is better?" — it's "when does each make sense?" Farm before GS 37. Craft after GS 37. There is no scenario where farming bandit camps post-Grinder is the optimal choice. The material cost of crafting is trivial, and the time saved is enormous.

⚠️ Common Whetstone Mistakes

Every mistake below costs hours of unnecessary farming. Learn from players who already made them.

❌ Farming bandit camps past GS 40

Why it happens: Habit. Players find a farming route that works and keep running it without realizing a better option unlocked.

Better solution: The moment the Grinder is built, switch to passive crafting. If you catch yourself running a bandit camp loop past GS 40, stop — you're wasting time.

❌ Delaying the Grinder unlock

Why it happens: Players get sidetracked by exploration, base building, or optional bosses and postpone the Quincey fight.

Better solution: Quincey (GS 37) should be a priority kill. The Grinder unlocks not just Whetstone crafting but also Gem Dust production — two critical resource pipelines. Delay this and you're handicapping your entire mid-game.

❌ Running out of Whetstones right before Iron upgrades

Why it happens: No stockpile. Players craft exactly what they need for the current upgrade and have zero buffer.

Better solution: Always maintain 10 Whetstones in reserve. The Iron-tier upgrade chain requires 20-30 Whetstones total — running out halfway through means stopping progression to go craft more. Avoid the interruption.

❌ Using all Copper Ingots for Whetstones, neglecting weapon crafts

Why it happens: Over-correction. Players discover crafting is efficient and convert all Copper into Whetstones.

Better solution: Whetstones are a means to an end — weapon upgrades are the end. Always craft your weapon first, then spend leftover Copper Ingots on Whetstones.

❌ Ignoring Stone Dust as a bottleneck

Why it happens: Stone feels worthless. Players stop picking it up after the early game.

Better solution: Pick up raw Stone whenever you see it. A stack of 100 Stone grinds into 100 Stone Dust, which crafts into 100 Whetstones (assuming you have the Copper). Stone is the silent bottleneck — you'll run out of Stone Dust long before you run out of Copper Ingots.

❓ Whetstone FAQ

Why can't I craft Whetstones in V Rising?
You need to unlock the Grinder workstation first. The Grinder is unlocked after defeating Quincey the Bandit King (GS 37) in Dunley Farmlands. Build the Grinder, then craft Whetstones using 1 Copper Ingot + 1 Stone Dust. If you have a Grinder but still can't craft, check that both ingredients are in your inventory — Stone Dust is obtained by grinding raw Stone at the same Grinder.
Should I farm or craft Whetstones?
Craft them. Once you unlock the Grinder (GS 37), crafting is always more efficient than farming bandit camps. The materials — Copper Ingots and Stone Dust — are passive byproducts of normal gameplay. Farming bandit camps for Whetstone drops should only be done for your first 10-15 Whetstones before unlocking the recipe. Full decision framework →
Where is the fastest Whetstone farming route?
The western Farbane Woods bandit loop: start at the Farbane Waygate → clear the western cliff camp → Ancient Village (northwest) → Bandit Camp Overlook (north). One loop: ~8 minutes, 3-5 Whetstones, 60-80 Copper Ore, 8-12 Gem Stones. Full route details →
Can merchants sell Whetstones?
No. There are no vendors in V Rising that sell Whetstones. You must either loot them from bandit camps (early game) or craft them at the Grinder (mid game onward).
How many Whetstones do I need total?
A full playthrough requires approximately 80-120 Whetstones. The biggest consumers are Merciless Copper weapons (~16-20 for a full set) and Iron-tier upgrades (~20-30). Plan to craft at least 60 Whetstones passively. Full recipe breakdown →
When should I stop farming Whetstones?
The moment you build the Grinder (GS 37). Active farming past this point is a time-waste. Switch to passive crafting: collect Copper Ore and Stone during normal play, craft Whetstones at the Grinder when you return to base. Mid-game transition guide →
Can bosses drop Whetstones?
No. V Blood bosses do not drop Whetstones. Whetstones come from bandit enemies, bandit camp chests, or crafting at the Grinder.
Is crafting Whetstones cheaper than farming?
Yes. 1 Copper Ingot (20 Copper Ore) + 1 Stone Dust costs almost nothing — both materials are abundant and collected passively. Active farming yields 3-5 Whetstones per 8-minute loop. The opportunity cost of farming is far higher than the material cost of crafting. Full comparison →
What unlocks the Whetstone recipe?
The Grinder workstation, unlocked after defeating Quincey the Bandit King (GS 37) in Dunley Farmlands. Recipe: 1 Copper Ingot + 1 Stone Dust = 1 Whetstone. No additional blueprint or boss drop is needed.
Can I automate Whetstone production with servants?
Not directly. Servants cannot craft Whetstones. However, Worker-blood servants on resource-gathering missions can collect Copper Ore and Stone passively. Send them on 2-hour or 6-hour resource runs, then craft Whetstones yourself when you return. This is the closest to automation available. Servant guide →
What's the Whetstone stack size?
500. You can carry up to 500 Whetstones in a single inventory slot. This is more than enough for any playthrough — you'll never hit the stack limit in normal gameplay.
Do Whetstones work on all weapon types?
No. Whetstones are specifically used for Copper-tier (Merciless) and Iron-tier weapon upgrades, plus certain Grinder recipes. They are not used for Dark Silver, Sanguine, or Oakveil-tier weapons. Whetstones are a Tier 2 material — their relevance peaks at GS 20-55.

Stop Farming. Start Crafting. Keep Progressing.

Whetstones are a progression gate, not a lifestyle. Farm your first dozen, unlock the Grinder, and never look back. Bookmark this page — we update resource data with every V Rising patch.
